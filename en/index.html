The apartment

Old Nice character,
quiet on the courtyard

The building dates from the 18th century and the duplex has kept its proportions: high ceilings, thick walls that hold the cool through summer, and windows facing the inner courtyard rather than the street. You sleep in a quiet that is rare at this address.

The 54 m² are spread over two floors. The living room and kitchen occupy the first; a staircase leads up to a double bed and a single bed. Two sofa beds measuring 140 × 190 cm complete the arrangement, one in the living room and one in the study bedroom — enough for five guests without anyone drawing the short straw.

This is an apartment for people who want to live in the neighbourhood rather than look at it: the market in the morning, the port terraces in the evening, the Promenade des Anglais at the end of the street.

Frequently asked

Good to know

How many guests can the duplex sleep?

Up to five. Upstairs there is a double bed and a single bed, and two sofa beds measuring 140 × 190 cm are found in the living room and the study bedroom.

Is the apartment noisy?

No. The duplex faces the building's inner courtyard rather than the street, which is one of its real advantages in such a lively district.

Where can I park?

The Lympia car park is directly opposite the building. It is paid parking and requires no advance booking.

Can I work from the apartment?

Yes. The WiFi runs on fibre and the study bedroom has a dedicated work corner, which suits longer stays and remote work.

Why book direct rather than through Airbnb or Booking?

Because platform commissions do not apply. For identical dates, the direct rate is the better one, and you deal with your host directly before and during your stay.
